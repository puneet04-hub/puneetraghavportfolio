IRCTC Redesign

Reducing drop-off in IRCTC's Tatkal ticket booking

A redesigned mobile booking flow that takes travellers from login to a confirmed ticket in fewer steps, with no ads in the way.

View Prototype
Year2026
Duration2 Weeks
RoleSolo UI/UX Designer
The problem

Tatkal windows last minutes, yet IRCTC logs users out and fills the flow with ads

The solution

A one-step login and an ad-free booking flow until the ticket is confirmed

What I designed

Five changes that remove friction from the booking journey

11
1

One-step login

The complicated sign-in is replaced with a phone number and OTP, or Google. Users log in once and stay in.

22
2

Ads in their own section

Promotions and services live in separate sections on the home screen, so nothing competes with the booking card.

33
3

Best train and seats, faster

A date strip shows seat status at a glance, and quick filters for Tatkal, Available and AC narrow the list instantly.

4
4

Customer support in one tap

A dedicated support section offers IRCTC Care and the Railway Helpline, available 24/7 for booking and refund queries.

5
5

A simpler Connect with IRCTC page

A clear heading, a short form and inline password rules make linking an IRCTC account quick and error-free.

Background

The app fails when travellers need it most

Imagine booking a Tatkal ticket home for a festival. The window opens, and the app logs you out again and again.

58% of surveyed Tatkal users succeeded 25% of the time or less, and 32% preferred a ticket agent over booking online.

Source: LocalCircles national Tatkal survey, June 2025
Research

I compared four booking apps and interviewed eight travellers

Interviewees were a mix of students and working professionals who book trains regularly.

Competitor takeaways

  • Direct login and a simple booking flow win
  • Too many ads and hidden offers hurt
  • Opportunity: festive offers and connecting-train suggestions

What users told me

  • No clear Tatkal or general booking path
  • Too many ads and promotions
  • No connecting or interchange suggestions
  • No real-time location and no customer support
Define

Meet Akshat

An engineering student travelling from Delhi to Patna for Holi, Diwali and Chhath, when trains are packed.

Goals

  • No ads or promotions during booking
  • One-time login
  • A simple process

Frustrations

  • Too many ads and promotions
  • Logged out several times while booking Tatkal
  • No proper customer support
How might we

Give users a one-step login and an ad-free booking flow until the ticket is confirmed?

Information architecture

One short path from login to confirmation

Login, availability, booking, payment and confirmation, with IRCTC Care reachable from any step.

Visual design

Clean, calm and reassuring

A sky-blue and violet palette, the Inter typeface, large buttons and clear status colours keep booking fast and easy to trust.

AvailableWaitlistNot available
Final thoughts

Simple beats feature-rich when time is short

What I learned

Users always want a simple process. Shorter details save them time.

Next steps

  • Suggest connecting or alternate trains below the booking
  • Test the prototype with users and measure booking time
