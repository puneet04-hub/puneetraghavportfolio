About Me

My journey into UI/UX design is driven by a passion for solving real user problems through clean visual hierarchy, thoughtful interaction design, and scalable design systems. Currently pursuing my B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (AI & ML) at Vishveshwarya Group of Institutions (2023–2027), I bridge technical feasibility with human-centered design.

During my internship as a UI/UX Designer at Aether AI Pvt. Ltd. in Gurugram, I collaborated directly with developers to translate user research and app flows into wireframes, interactive Figma prototypes, and reusable UI component libraries for mobile applications.

Whether conducting user journey mapping, building responsive Auto Layout systems in Figma, or refining micro-interactions, I believe great design makes complex digital products feel effortless, intuitive, and welcoming.
