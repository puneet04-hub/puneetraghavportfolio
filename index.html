About Me

Designing products
people understand.

I’m Puneet Raghav, a Product Designer focused on creating useful, intuitive, and visually engaging digital experiences. I approach design as a balance of user needs, business goals, visual clarity, and practical implementation.

I work with Figma, FigJam, Adobe XD, wireframing, interactive prototyping, responsive design, user research, and UI animation. My experience includes designing mobile applications, websites, and digital platforms with a focus on usability, visual hierarchy, and consistent design systems.

I’m especially interested in transforming complex problems into simple product experiences that help people accomplish their goals with confidence.
